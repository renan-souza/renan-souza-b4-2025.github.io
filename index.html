About. I lead the design, implementation, and deployment of intelligent data and AI architectures to accelerate discovery. With 10+ years at IBM and ORNL, I specialize in translating the uncertain, abstract requirements of highly specialized domains (Energy, Chemistry, Biology, Climate) into concrete, production-scale systems.
My expertise is on low-latency, observable, provenance- and metadata-first architectures bridging Edge instruments, OpenShift/Kubernetes cloud clusters, and leadership-class supercomputers. Integrating data from SQL and NoSQL databases, cloud storages, and parallel file systems into data streaming backbones, I facilitate the explainability, transparency, and reproducibility that accelerate data-to-discovery cycles for massive-scale workflows—spanning AI/ML, LLM-driven or Agentic AI, and parallel scientific applications—within highly scalable systems.

Areas of Expertise

  • Agentic AI workflows and intelligent steering
  • Architecting for the Edge-Cloud-HPC continuum
  • Provenance-driven observability and data lineage
  • High-performance data engineering (SQL, NoSQL, Streaming, Parallel File Systems)

Current: Workflows, AI, and Data Engineering Research Scientist at Oak Ridge National Laboratory
